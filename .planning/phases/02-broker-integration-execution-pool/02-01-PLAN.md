<objective>
Automatically size the worker process pool based on available resources and configure RabbitMQ prefetch to match.

Purpose: Prevents RabbitMQ from starving other consumers by pushing too many messages to a single worker pool that cannot process them immediately. By matching prefetch to process pool size, jobs stay in the RabbitMQ queue until a worker is genuinely available.
Output: A resource-aware `JobManager` with bound throughput.
</objective>
